## Initial Business Problem Analysis
Initial Business Request
ABC Bank has received an increasing number of customer complaints related to ATM cash withdrawals.
Some customers report that their cash withdrawal transactions are declined even though they believe they have sufficient money in their accounts.
Other customers report that they are unsure why their transaction was declined.
The Customer Service department has asked the IT department to investigate whether the ATM cash withdrawal process can be improved.

## What do we know?
Based on the initial business request, we know that:
- Some ABC Bank customers have encountered problems when withdrawing cash at ATMs.
- ABC Bank has received an increasing number of customer complaints.
- Some customers report that their withdrawal was declined even though they believed they had sufficient money.
- Some customers do not understand why their withdrawal was declined.
- Customer Service has asked the IT department to investigate the ATM cash withdrawal process.

## What we don't know?
For example:
- Whether the affected ATMs belong to ABC Bank or other banks.
- Whether the withdrawals occurred in Switzerland or abroad.
- Which currencies were involved.
- Which types of cards were affected.
- How many customers were affected.
- If all customers are affected or a specific group
- When the increase in complaints started.
- Whether all declined transactions were incorrect.
- What reasons caused the transactions to be declined.
- What information customers currently receive when a transaction is declined.

## Initial questions
The following questions should be clarified during the initial investigation:
- Which customers and transactions are affected?
- Where do the affected transactions occur?
- Are the affected ATMs operated by ABC Bank or another bank?
- Are there specific countries or currencies involved?
- What types of cards are affected?
- How many complaints have been received?
- When did the increase in complaints begin?
- What are the recorded reasons for declined transactions?
- What information is currently provided to customers when a withdrawal is declined?
- Are the transactions being declined incorrectly, or is the main problem that customers do not understand the reason?

## Initial assumptions
At this stage, no solution should be defined.

Further investigation is required to understand the problem and determine whether the issue is related to:
- incorrect transaction declines
- missing or unclear information provided to customers
- a specific ATM, country, card type or transaction scenario
- another underlying issue

These assumptions need to be validated during the next stages of the analysis.

## After initial assumptions
